How to read this
Chance
The halfway point between bid and ask. A YES contract pays $1 if the answer is yes, so a price of 42¢ roughly means the market thinks there's a 42% chance.
Bid
The highest price someone is offering to buy YES at.
Ask
The lowest price YES is offered for sale at. Kalshi only lists bids, so this is 100¢ minus the best NO bid.
Spread
Ask minus bid. A small spread means an active market; a wide one means a few cents' disagreement or thin trading.
Size
How many contracts are available at the bid or ask price.
Updated
How long ago the worker saved this price. Stale means its age exceeds the configured limit, which defaults to 2 minutes.
Gaps
Unusually long gaps between collections break the chart lines. Missing prices are left empty.
